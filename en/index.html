01 / ACADEMIC PROFILE

Scientific research with an industrial perspective

I am an Associate Professor in Manufacturing Engineering at the Department of Mechanics, University of Córdoba, Spain. My teaching and research focus on understanding materials, surfaces and manufacturing processes through experimental investigation, characterization, and optimization.

My work aims to connect research findings with industrial needs, from non-stick coating performance and mold release to additive manufacturing, experimental design and sustainable material solutions.
