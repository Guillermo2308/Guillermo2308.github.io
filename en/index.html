01 / INTRODUCTION

Scientific experience connected with industrial practice

I am Guillermo Guerrero Vacas, an Associate Professor in Manufacturing Engineering at the Department of Mechanics, University of Córdoba.

My teaching and research focus on understanding and improving materials, surfaces and manufacturing processes. I work particularly on functional coatings, materials characterization, experimental optimization and advanced manufacturing technologies.

My aim is to build productive connections between scientific research and industrial needs, applying evidence from research projects and publications to new engineering problems.
