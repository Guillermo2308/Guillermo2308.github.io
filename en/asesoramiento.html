TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER · APPLIED RESEARCH

Scientific and Technical Collaboration

Collaboration in diagnosing and experimentally investigating industrial challenges related to functional surfaces, materials and manufacturing processes. The scope of each activity is agreed according to the problem, objectives and available resources.

Coating selection

Define functional requirements and compare coating systems based on release performance, adhesion, temperature, chemical compatibility and maintenance.

Mold release and adhesion

Investigate contact materials, mold surfaces, roughness, wettability and process conditions relevant to separation.

Coating removal and refurbishment

Compare removal strategies and evaluate their impact on the underlying substrate.

Characterization and durability

Plan and interpret experiments addressing surface properties, wear, erosion and functional stability.

Manufacturing optimization

Metrology, machining, additive manufacturing and design of experiments for manufacturing challenges.

Applied research projects

Research questions, experimental protocols, objective evaluation and transfer of validated results.

Industry collaboration and university research

Industrial collaboration: Tecnimacor

I collaborate and provide scientific and technical advice to Tecnimacor, a Spanish company specializing in the industrial application of technical and non-stick coatings. Our collaboration concerns functional surfaces, release performance and the evaluation of coating solutions.
