Coatings · 2018

Release of polyurethane foam from industrial molds

Challenge: Reduce dependence on liquid release agents while maintaining release performance.

Approach: Experimental coating comparison and monitoring of production cycles.

Result: The selected PFA coating enabled approximately 1,500 cycles without release agent, with few incidents under the study conditions.

Polymers · 2025

Hybrid polymer–aluminium bonding by 3D printing

Challenge: Improve adhesion between extrusion-printed polymers and a metal substrate.

Approach: Surface treatments, primer application, and tensile and shear testing.

Result: PETG showed higher bond strength than PLA under the tested conditions.

Journal of Materials Research and Technology · 2024

Additive manufacturing of turning inserts

Challenge: Evaluate the feasibility of producing customized metallic inserts by material extrusion.

Approach: Manufacture of Cr–Mo steel inserts and turning tests under different cutting conditions.

Result: Feed and depth of cut influenced wear more than cutting speed; the inserts operated within a process range comparable to commercial tools.

Read the study ↗

Materials · 2024

Design and optimization of Zn–Al wire drawing

Challenge: Improve industrial drawing sequences for ZnAl15% wire used in spray metallization.

Approach: Analytical modeling and numerical simulation supported by experimental validation.

Result: The models provide a basis for assessing process limits and comparing redesign and optimization strategies.
