RESEARCH AREA

Sustainable Materials

Technological valorization of low-commercial-value sheep wool through composite materials and functional applications, linking manufacturing engineering with circular-economy research.

Wool characterization

Studying the relationship between incoming fibre properties, processing conditions and the performance of the resulting materials.

Natural-fibre composites

Research on matrix systems, wool-reinforced formulations and experimental evaluation of mechanical behaviour.

Thermal and hygrothermal properties

Developing technological suitability criteria for insulation and other sustainable material applications, supported by testing and material characterization.

Research direction

The approach combines experimental materials engineering with multidisciplinary collaboration, seeking to identify measurable fibre characteristics that can predict processing performance and product suitability.

Research proposals awaiting an award decision are not presented as funded projects.
