02 / UNIVERSITY TEACHING

University teaching

Associate Professor (Profesor Titular de Universidad) in Manufacturing Process Engineering, Department of Mechanics, Higher Polytechnic School of Córdoba. Teaching experience spans former technical engineering qualifications and present-day undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.

Undergraduate

Mechanical Engineering

Manufacturing Engineering; Manufacturing Processes, Metrology and Quality Control; Computer-Aided Manufacturing (previous courses).

Postgraduate

Master's Degree in Industrial Engineering

Manufacturing Technology; Handling and Transport in Manufacturing.

Other programmes and previous teaching

Electrical Engineering and Industrial Electronics Engineering degrees (Manufacturing Engineering); former Technical Industrial Engineering programmes in mechanics, electricity and industrial electronics. Previous subjects include mechanical technology, CNC programming, CAD/CAM, machine design and metrology.
