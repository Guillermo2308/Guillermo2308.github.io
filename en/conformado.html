RESEARCH AREA

Metal Forming and Process Modelling

Single-point incremental forming (SPIF), mold manufacturing, dimensional accuracy, pre-coated metal sheets and predictive process models.

Incremental forming and functional surfaces

Forming sheets pre-coated with PTFE connects materials processing and surface engineering. The experimental challenge is twofold: producing the intended geometry while evaluating any changes in thickness, roughness, wettability and functional coating performance.

Research in this area considers forming strategies, sheet behaviour, process parameter optimization and predictive modelling, including machine-learning methods for geometrical accuracy.
