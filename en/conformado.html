RESEARCH AREA

Metal Forming and Process Modelling

Single-point incremental forming (SPIF), mold manufacturing, dimensional accuracy, pre-coated metal sheets and predictive process models.

Incremental forming and functional surfaces

Forming sheets pre-coated with PTFE connects materials processing and surface engineering. The experimental challenge is twofold: producing the intended geometry while evaluating any changes in thickness, roughness, wettability and functional coating performance.

Research in this area considers forming strategies, sheet behaviour, process parameter optimization and predictive modelling, including machine-learning methods for geometrical accuracy.

Selected contributions

2018 · Manufacturing of Non-Stick Molds from Pre-Painted Aluminum Sheets via Single Point Incremental Forming

Development of non-stick molds from precoated aluminium sheets, assessing the effect of process parameters on geometric accuracy.

2019 · Incremental forming of non-stick pre-coated sheets

SPIF of PTFE-coated aluminized steel, using a protective sheet to reduce damage during forming.

2021 · SVM-based prediction of geometric accuracy

Predictive modelling of geometrical accuracy based on 27 experimental molds, exploring the influence of process parameters.

2022 · Influence of SPIF on surface properties of PTFE-coated sheets

Experimental evaluation of the changes in coating thickness, hardness, roughness, durability and wettability.

2026 · Flexible tooling through 3D extrusion printing

Combination of 3D-printed tooling and incremental forming for advanced manufacturing applications, including surgical implants.
