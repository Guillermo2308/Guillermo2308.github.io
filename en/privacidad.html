Data, purposes and legal basis

Enquiries may include a name, email address, organisation and details of the subject. The form prepares a message in the visitor’s email application; it does not directly transmit or store fields in a website database. Messages received are processed to respond and manage collaboration. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in handling requested professional communications (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) or, where applicable, steps requested before entering a contract (Article 6(1)(b)). Providing data is voluntary, but a reply requires contact details. Sensitive data are not requested and these data are not used for automated decisions or profiling.

The website may display names, affiliations and bibliographic references from publications, repositories and public professional sources to document scientific activity, based on the legitimate interest in disseminating and correctly attributing contributions. Individuals may request review or object.

Retention and recipients

Enquiries are retained as long as necessary to resolve and follow them up, then deleted or retained only as required by legal obligations or potential liabilities. No sale of data or personalised advertising use is envisaged. Technology and email providers, authorities where legally required, and strictly necessary collaborators may be involved where a legal basis exists.
