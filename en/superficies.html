RESEARCH AREA

Surface Engineering and Functional Coatings

This research area investigates how coating chemistry, surface topography, substrate preparation, and manufacturing processes affect functional properties. Particular attention is paid to non-stick performance, durability, surface characterization and refurbishment.

Non-stick coatings and industrial molds

Experimental evaluation of fluoropolymer and commercial coating systems for mold-release applications, including aluminium molds used in polyurethane-foam manufacturing.

Coating removal and refurbishment

Laser ablation, thermal processes and dry abrasives; comparing coating-removal effectiveness and the preservation of metallic substrates.

Wettability and surface functionality

Hydrophobic and superhydrophobic surfaces, contact-angle measurements, surface treatments and microtexturing.

Durability and erosion

Wear, surface damage and the evolution of functional performance under repeated use and erosive conditions.

Sol-gel alternatives

Experimental comparisons of ceramic sol-gel and fluoropolymer systems, without assuming universal equivalence between their anti-adhesion and durability performance.

Surface engineering meets incremental forming

Research on sheets pre-coated with PTFE connects surface functionalization and single-point incremental forming. The challenge is not just to manufacture the target shape: it is also to preserve, quantify or understand the changes in coating functionality after deformation.
