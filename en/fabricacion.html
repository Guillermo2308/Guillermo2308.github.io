RESEARCH AREA

Additive Manufacturing and Machining

Research in advanced manufacturing, process parameters, material characterization and the relationship between manufacturing routes and component performance.

Metal additive manufacturing

Investigation of metallic components produced by additive methods, including post-process machining, dimensional and surface characterization, and the assessment of process-related properties.

Polymer and hybrid manufacturing

Fused filament fabrication (FFF), polymer–metal bonding, surface preparation and the mechanical evaluation of hybrid material systems.

Machining and process optimization

Cutting parameters, tool behaviour, dimensional quality and design of experiments, including statistical strategies for optimizing manufacturing processes.

Research approach

Manufacturing processes are evaluated through experiments, mechanical testing, metrology and surface measurements. A key objective is to identify the variables that control dimensional accuracy, surface quality, adhesion or the performance of the finished component.

Further scientific profiles and quantitative results will be added progressively as they are reviewed against the original publications.
